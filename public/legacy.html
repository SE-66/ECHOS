ECHOS is a synthetic research demonstration, not a financial adviser or a deployed AI model. It checks explicitly coded rules, not business desirability. “Verified” is selected by the user; no underlying documents are authenticated. The impact breakdown is a mathematical allocation across changed inputs, not proof of real-world causality.
