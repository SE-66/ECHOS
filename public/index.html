Decision Workspace

Build and investigate a business decision from evidence to competing actions. All five analysis stages share the same case.

The internal decision bank is not a catalogue you must browse. ECHOS assesses your submitted case and reports incomplete coverage.

1. Case and evidence

Identify the company situation, dates, source records and claims. Every claim should distinguish fact, estimate, assumption or unknown.

Load the example to explore a complete case.

2. Context and obligations

Annotate which claims are existing, proposed, historical or hypothetical. Authority remains subject to review.

3. Connected relationships

Model relationships from the claims to affected financial and organizational variables. Unverified links are warnings, not causal proof.

4. Alternatives and scenarios

Define options, hard requirements, objectives, scenarios and observed values. Passing stated criteria is not a global decision approval.

Criterion-to-evidence links

5. Documentary passages

Attach source excerpts, effective dates and possible implications. Classifications are candidate interpretations for expert review.

An excerpt is not proof of a legally binding obligation. The system does not determine which contract provision takes precedence.

6. Consolidated assessment

Run one analysis across the five connected components. Inspect the decision comparison, source gaps and passage interpretation in the same report.
