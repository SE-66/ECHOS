# ECHOS Step 5 — Human–AI Decision Transition Evaluation (research prototype)

## Purpose
Turn Step 4's rule-based alternative assessments into paired baseline/changed cases for measuring observable human and AI responses. The research does **not** treat a model's own rationale or confidence as proof of correctness.

## Experimental conditions
Human only; AI only; human assisted by AI; AI assisted by human. Record participants, model/version, prompts, information exposure, decision responsibility, timestamps, repeats, and overrides **outside this prototype**. No genuine human-subject research or online model integration is claimed.

## Supported outcomes
- Appropriate action adaptation when reference feasibility assessments change
- Reassessment with justified stable feasibility when material inputs change without crossing a rule boundary
- Stable response where there is no decision-relevant change
- Evidence abstention where a hard requirement cannot be judged due to disputed or missing evidence
- Incorrect response and missing response counts, separately

## Local workflow
1. Author an experiment design JSON with `pairs`: each requires `id`, `baseline` and `changed` Step 4 model objects, and `material_change` (boolean). The validity of this last classification must be independently reviewed. The Step 4 models encode supplied alternatives, criteria, scenarios, and observations.
2. Run `node experiment-cli.js prepare design.json public-cases.json private-key.json` from the repository root. **Never publish private-key.json or use the preparation API on a public endpoint.**
3. Send the cases only to consenting participants or approved AI evaluations. Do not include the key, expected labels, or generated scores. Participants should give one record per case: `{ "case_id": "...", "assessments": { "base/accept": "passes_stated_rules", "base/negotiate": "unresolved" } }`, using the exact scenario/alternative identifiers and one of `passes_stated_rules`, `blocked_by_stated_rule`, `unresolved`.
4. Run `node experiment-cli.js score private-key.json answers.json report.json`. Review case-level mistakes and denominators. Treat missing responses as missing, not failures silently dropped.

## Why this is a research prototype, not a validated metric
The reference is generated by Step 4, which checks *supplied* rules; the true completeness, provenance, and applicability of those rules require independently expert-reviewed reference cases. `material_change` is a declared research label, not inferred by the software. This harness does not yet operationalize ACBS or DCAG as validated scales, infer mental understanding, attribute real-world financial causes, or justify delegation of decision authority. Statistical confidence intervals, pre-registration, repeated runs, blinding randomization, leakage controls, and independent replication are still required. Stage 1–4 integration is not automatic.
